# CSFRBench: recorded results

These are the results of the training runs of the paper, as the package holds them
(`csfrbench.record`). Each value is the arithmetic mean of the AULC over the target instances with the
half-width of its two-sided 95% Student-t interval. Lower is better. An AULC integrates the test NMSE over
$n = 2^9, \dots, 2^{15}$ ($2^{11}, \dots, 2^{17}$ on the depth-5 tree), so values are comparable within a
column only. The depth-5 tree and the workloads with 8 inputs have different grids and backbones.
